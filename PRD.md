# PRD — Masjid e Mohammadi website

**Vision:** Masjid ka ek proper public website — app ki shakal nahi, real
full-width website jo community aur visitors dono ke liye kaam kare.

**Goals:** prayer times turant dikhein · masjid ka taaruf · app ka download ·
YouTube videos · contact/location.

**Target users:** local musallis, naye visitors, aur wo log jo Google se masjid
dhoondh kar aate hain.

**User stories**
- As a visitor, main aaj ke prayer times foran dekh sakoon (meri location se).
- As a user, main app open kar sakoon / APK download kar sakoon.
- As a viewer, main naye bayan dekh sakoon.
- As a visitor, main masjid ka pata/contact dhoond sakoon.

**Features (must):** live prayer times (Aladhan, GPS + IP fallback) · About +
stats · 4 feature cards · YouTube (real channel videos + in-page player) ·
contact + Google Maps · app download band (APK + web app).

**Non-functional:** mobile-first responsive · arctic glass brand match · fast
(vanilla, no frameworks) · offline-friendly site SW · SEO + OG.

**Success:** Shan bole "yeh proper website hai" + install/web/YouTube sab kaam kare.

**Out of scope (abhi):** admin panel backend, donations/payments, multi-language,
native iOS.
