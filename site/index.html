About the Masjid
“Beshak Allah ke ghar hi wo jagah hai jahan uska zikr kiya jaata hai.”

Masjid e Mohammadi ek community markaz hai — namaz, taleem aur khidmat. Har umar ke logon ke liye khula darwaza. Yahan aap sukoon paayenge, ilm seekhenge aur bhai-chare ke saath rahenge.

Hamari koshish hai ki technology ke zariye masjid aur aapke beech ka rabta hamesha bana rahe — chahe aap ghar par hon ya safar me.
